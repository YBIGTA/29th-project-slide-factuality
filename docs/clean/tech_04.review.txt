[p1 각주]   ABSTRACT
[p1 각주]   This study examines the feasibility of real-time early detection of combustion instability using a ResNet-18
[p1 각주]   model trained on recurrence plot images derived from dynamic pressure signals. Experiments were
[p1 각주]   conducted in a hydrogen-fueled premixed combustor by gradually increasing the fuel flow rate from three
[p1 각주]   stable initial velocities, which induced several high-frequency instability modes. A model trained on a
[p1 각주]   single mode could detect instability onset, but its performance depended on the frequency difference
[p1 각주]   between the trained and target instability modes. A combined-frequency model showed effective detection
[p1 각주]   performance, although improper frequency selection caused overly sensitive responses in stable regions.
[p1 각주]   These results indicate that robust early detection requires not only multi-band training, but also careful
[p1 각주]   selection of representative instability frequencies.
[p1 각주]   Key Words : Combustion instability, Hydrogen, Early detection, Deep learning, Recurrence plot
